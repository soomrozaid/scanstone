## Cancelling: two pages, two answers

If you do pay, read the cancellation rules before you need them, because QRCG's own pages disagree. The FAQ on its pricing page says "an informal email would be sufficient" and that the notice period is 30 days before the end of the billing cycle. Its help centre article on [how to cancel](https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665399659277-How-do-I-cancel-my-QRCG-by-Bitly-subscription), updated in September 2026, says to cancel from the Billing page at least one day before your renewal date, that a renewal can't be cancelled once it has gone through, and that refunds aren't given for unused time.

The safe reading is the stricter one: cancel from the Billing page well before the renewal date. QRCG doesn't send a confirmation email, so take a screenshot of the Payment Details section, which shows the date you cancelled and the date the plan ends.

After that, your dynamic codes keep redirecting until the paid period ends and for 30 more days, but you can't edit them in those 30 days. Then the account is locked and the codes go to a service page. Paying again brings them back, and per its [reactivation guide](https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665090677005-How-can-I-reactivate-my-QR-Codes) it has to be the original account; a new account or a new code with the same link won't revive a printed code.
