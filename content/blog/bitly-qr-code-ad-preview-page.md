If people scan your Bitly QR code and land on a "destination preview" page with an ad before they reach your site, that's Bitly's free plan working as designed. Bitly shows that page on links and QR codes from free accounts. If the code came from a free Bitly account, paying for any Bitly plan removes it, and the cheapest is Core at $120 USD a year. If the link was made without a Bitly account, the ad can't be removed from it at all, and the only fix is a new code.

## Why the ad page is there

Bitly's help article [Why are there ads on my links?](https://support.bitly.com/hc/en-us/articles/32874287800333-Why-are-there-ads-on-my-links) says it is "now using ads on free accounts to continue delivering free plans at no cost", through "an interstitial page that includes a preview of the link destination and an ad". Its [pricing page](https://bitly.com/pages/pricing) labels the free plan "With Ads".

The change reached links that were already out there. When Bitly told free users about it in February 2025, the notice said "your Bitly links and QR Codes may show a preview page before directing your audience to the destination URL", as [Coywolf reported](https://coywolf.com/news/social-media/bitly-adds-interstitial-ads-to-shortened-urls-unlocking-new-revenue-stream/). So a code that went straight through when you printed it can show the page today.

For a link in an email or a post, the extra page is an annoyance. For a printed QR code it's worse. People scanning a menu, a flyer or a box can't see why there's an ad, some think the code is broken or unsafe, and you can't swap the link on paper you've already handed out.

## If the code is in your free Bitly account

Bitly's help article says the fix is to upgrade. Its February 2025 notice put it this way: "You can remove the preview page experience from your links and QR Codes at any time by selecting a paid Bitly plan." The codes you already printed keep their address, so nothing needs reprinting. On the [pricing page](https://bitly.com/pages/pricing), checked November 24, 2026:

- **Core** is $10 USD a month, sold only as one annual charge of $120. It also lets you change where codes point, 5 times a month.
- **Growth** is $29 a month billed yearly ($348), or $35 a month on monthly billing (checked November 16, 2026). It adds your own domain.

Plans renew automatically. Bitly says free accounts show the ad page, so expect it to come back if you later drop to the free plan. Paying for Core is worth it if the code is printed on something expensive or hard to replace, like signage, packaging or a large print run. For a stack of flyers, a reprint may cost less than a year of Core.

## If it was made without an account

Bitly's [anonymous link FAQ](https://support.bitly.com/hc/en-us/articles/32500359495565-Free-Short-Link-Generator-Anonymous-Link-FAQ) (updated November 6, 2026) is clear about these:

- They "will still work, but they will display a preview of the destination along with advertisements."
- Upgrading "will remove the link preview and ads from new links you create, but your old links cannot be transferred into an account or upgraded."
- Its only fix is to make a new link in a paid account and "replace your old anonymous link with your new one wherever it's being used."

For a printed QR code, replacing the link means printing a new code. Paying Bitly doesn't fix the old one, so you don't have to stay with Bitly for the new one either.

## Common questions

### Why does my Bitly QR code show an ad?

Because it's a link from a free Bitly account or from Bitly's old no-account shortener. Bitly puts a "destination preview" page with an ad in front of those links before people reach your site.

### How do I remove the ad from my Bitly QR code?

If the code is in your own Bitly account, upgrade that account to any paid plan. The cheapest is Core, $120 USD a year billed annually. If the link was made without an account, it can't be removed; make a new code and reprint.

### Do I have to reprint my Bitly QR code?

Not if it's in a Bitly account you can pay for, since upgrading removes the page from existing links. You do have to reprint if it was made without an account, or if you'd rather not pay Bitly every year.

### Will the ad come back if I cancel Bitly?

Bitly says free accounts show the ad page, and its downgrade article says links keep working on the free plan. So the links should keep working, but expect the preview page to come back.

### Does Scanstone put ads or a preview page in front of my link?

No. A Scanstone code or scst.one link sends people straight to your address on every plan, including free and links made without an account.

### Is a static QR code free of ads forever?

Yes. A static code holds your address in the pattern itself, so the phone goes straight to your site. Nobody can add an ad later. It keeps working as long as your website does.
