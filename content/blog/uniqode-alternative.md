## Is Uniqode free?

Only for static codes. A static code holds your link in the pattern itself, so it keeps working no matter what happens to your account, but you can't change where it points or count scans. Editable, trackable codes need a paid plan after the trial.

The [pricing FAQ](https://www.uniqode.com/pricing) says the 14-day trial needs no card and you won't be charged when it ends. It also says that when the trial ends, "your dynamic QR Codes become inactive unless you upgrade to a paid plan." Its help article on [reactivating codes](https://docs.uniqode.com/en/articles/9297614-how-do-i-reactivate-my-qr-codes) says you have to upgrade the same account you made them in; a new account with a paid plan won't bring them back.

So if you made dynamic codes during the trial and printed them, they need a paid plan or a reprint. You can check a printed code by scanning it and reading the address your phone shows before you tap. If it's a short link on Uniqode's domain rather than your own website, it depends on a Uniqode plan.

## Scan limits

This is the part that is easy to miss. Every Uniqode plan below Business+ has a yearly scan allowance for the whole account: 50,000 on Essential, 1,000,000 on Core and 2,000,000 on Plus. The pricing FAQ says that if you reach it, "those QR Codes stop working, so people can no longer access the content behind them."

50,000 scans a year is about 137 a day. A menu code in a busy restaurant, or a code on product packaging, can pass that. Scanstone doesn't count scans against your plan: scans and redirects are unlimited on Free and Pro, and going over a plan limit never switches a code off.

## Cancelling Uniqode

Its help article on [cancelling](https://docs.uniqode.com/en/articles/1818577-canceling-your-subscription) says to cancel from Billing in the Account menu, and that email requests are not accepted. Your plan runs until the renewal date. After that, per its article on [what happens when you cancel](https://docs.uniqode.com/en/articles/17190927-what-happens-to-my-qr-codes-campaigns-and-data-if-i-cancel), dynamic codes and campaigns stop working, you lose access to the dashboard, and you can't make new static codes. Reactivating brings the dynamic codes back.

A few things to know before you rely on that:

- **Export first.** The cancelling article says Uniqode keeps your data for 60 days before deleting it, and that you can't export after you lose dashboard access.
- **Cancel early on a yearly plan.** Uniqode's [terms of service](https://www.uniqode.com/terms-of-service) (section 23.1) say either party can end the agreement with written notice no later than 90 days before a renewal term. The help centre describes a cancel button with no notice period. If you're on a yearly plan and want out, cancel well before the renewal date.
- **Refunds.** The pricing FAQ offers a 30-day money-back guarantee, and the cancelling article says you can ask for a refund of auto-renewal charges after you cancel.

Before cancelling, Uniqode suggests making static versions of any codes that must keep working. That is good advice, but a static code is a new pattern, so it only helps for things you haven't printed yet.

## Common questions

### Is Uniqode free?

Static QR codes are free. Dynamic codes, which you can edit and track, are free only during a 14-day trial. After that they become inactive until you buy a plan on the same account.

### Is Uniqode the same as Beaconstac?

Yes. Uniqode was called Beaconstac, and its website still lists Beaconstac as its other name. Older guides and reviews use the old name.

### How much does Uniqode cost?

Essential is $9 USD a month, Core $49 and Plus $99, all billed yearly on its pricing page. Business+ is priced by sales. A custom domain is a $500 a year add-on on Plus.

### What happens to Uniqode QR codes when you cancel?

They keep working until your renewal date, then dynamic codes stop and scanners no longer reach your content. Static codes keep working. Reactivating your account brings the dynamic codes back.

### What happens when you reach Uniqode's scan limit?

Uniqode says codes stop working when you reach your plan's yearly scan limit: 50,000 scans on Essential, 1,000,000 on Core and 2,000,000 on Plus. Its FAQ suggests upgrading to a plan with a higher limit if you expect more scans.

### Is there a cheaper alternative to Uniqode for editable QR codes?

Several QR tools have free plans or cheaper monthly plans. Scanstone's free plan has 3 editable QR codes or short links with no time limit and no scan cap, and Pro is $9 USD a month or $79 USD a year for 500. Whichever you pick, check what happens when you stop paying before you print.
