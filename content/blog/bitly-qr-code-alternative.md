## Where it gets costly for QR codes

The catch is editing. A dynamic QR code is useful because you can change where it points after it's printed: next season's menu, a new booking page, a fixed typo. On Bitly that's called a redirect, and the [redirect help article](https://support.bitly.com/hc/en-us/articles/7995084842765-How-do-I-redirect-a-Bitly-Code) says it's available on paid plans only. Each one counts toward a monthly redirect limit, and if the code has a link attached it also counts as a new link.

So on Bitly's free plan, a code points to the same place forever. If you cancel a paid plan, your codes keep scanning but they're frozen at their last destination until you pay again. For a restaurant that changes its menu link each season, or a business card that may need a new booking page, that freeze is the real cost.

Two smaller things to know:

- **No monthly option on the entry plan.** Core is billed $120 a year up front. Growth and Premium can be paid monthly at a higher price. All Bitly plans renew automatically, as its pricing page says.
- **Monthly limits, not totals.** Bitly counts new codes and links per month. Scanstone counts how many you have at once: 3 on Free, 500 on Pro.

## Switching from Bitly

You can't move a printed bit.ly code to another company. The pattern holds a bit.ly address, and only Bitly can forward it. There's no need to, though: those codes keep working on Bitly's free plan. A sensible split:

1. **Leave existing printed codes on Bitly.** Save any scan numbers you want before you drop to Free, since Free shows no scan history. Free-plan links also get Bitly's preview page with an ad in front of them; here's [what that page is and what removes it](/blog/bitly-qr-code-ad-preview-page).
2. **Before downgrading, point codes where they should stay.** On Free you can't change them afterwards.
3. **Make new codes wherever you'll keep them.** Scan a test print before a big run and check the address it opens.

If you used a complimentary domain from Bitly, read its downgrade article closely. It says Bitly won't renew that domain on a plan without custom domains, and links on it stop working when the registration runs out.

## Old links and the November 2026 hijacks

In mid-November 2026, people found that years-old bit.ly links in brand posts, such as a 2011 McDonald's tweet, had been pointed at unrelated pages. [Malwarebytes reports](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2026/11/hijacked-bitly-links-turn-old-brand-posts-into-offensive-redirects) that Bitly described it as a flaw involving expired links, suspended the accounts involved and is disabling the affected links. Bitly's own [account deletion article](https://support.bitly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059651871-What-happens-to-my-personal-information-links-and-QR-Codes-after-my-account-is-deleted) says bit.ly back-halves can't be reused.

The lesson applies to any short-link or QR service, ours included. Don't delete a link or code that's still printed or posted somewhere. Point it at a page that still makes sense instead, and scan your old printed codes once in a while to see where they go.

## Common questions

### Do Bitly QR codes expire?

No. Bitly says its QR codes never expire and stop only if you delete the code or its short link. Paid plans can set an expiry date on purpose, but codes don't expire on their own.

### Can I edit a Bitly QR code for free?

No. Changing where a Bitly QR code points is called a redirect, and Bitly offers it on paid plans only, starting with Core at $120 USD a year. Core includes 5 redirects a month.

### What happens to my Bitly QR codes if I cancel?

Codes on the bit.ly domain keep working after you drop to the free plan, but you can't change their destination or see their scan history. Codes on your own domain keep working while its DNS points to Bitly, though the certificate isn't renewed. Codes on a complimentary Bitly domain stop when that domain's registration expires.

### Is there a cheaper alternative to Bitly for editable QR codes?

Several QR tools let you edit codes for less, and some on a free plan. Scanstone's free plan has 3 editable codes or short links, and Pro is $9 USD a month or $79 USD a year for 500. Check what each company does when you stop paying before you print.

### Can I move my bit.ly QR codes to Scanstone?

Not the printed ones. A printed code holds a bit.ly address, so only Bitly can forward it. They keep working on Bitly's free plan, so leave them there and make new codes wherever you want to keep them.
