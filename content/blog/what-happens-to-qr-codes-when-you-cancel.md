It depends on the company that made the code, and on whether the code is static or dynamic. A static code keeps working no matter what, because your link is stored in the pattern itself. A dynamic code sends every scan through the company's server first, so it keeps working only as long as that company keeps forwarding it. Most QR companies stop forwarding dynamic codes when a free trial ends or a paid plan lapses. A few keep some or all of them running on a free plan. Here is what each company says in its own help pages.

Static codes are the exception everywhere. Every company above that mentions them says static codes are not affected by a trial ending or a cancellation.

## The details, company by company

### QRCG by Bitly

Its help centre [says](https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665013224205-I-received-a-message-saying-that-my-QR-Codes-will-be-deactivated-What-does-that-mean) dynamic codes created during the 14-day free trial are deactivated at the end of the trial, and anyone who scans them lands on a service page. For paid plans, its [cancellation article](https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/12934511873165-What-happens-to-my-account-and-QR-Codes-if-I-cancel-my-subscription-renewal) says dynamic codes stay active for 30 days starting on the last day of your contract, and that you can't edit, download or see stats for them in that time. It also says cancellations must be made at least one day before the renewal date. To bring codes back, you have to upgrade the original account; a new account won't do it, per its [reactivation guide](https://support.qr-code-generator.com/hc/en-us/articles/7665090677005-How-can-I-reactivate-my-QR-Codes). Its prices, and the two different cancellation instructions on its own site, are in [Is qr-code-generator.com free?](/blog/is-qr-code-generator-com-free).

### QR TIGER

The free plan includes 3 dynamic codes with a 500-scan limit each. Its help centre [says](https://qrtiger.helpscoutdocs.com/article/201-is-using-qr-tiger-free) that once the limit is reached, the code stops working. For paid plans, its [cancellation article](https://qrtiger.helpscoutdocs.com/article/97-what-happens-if-my-paid-subscription-expires-or-if-i-cancel-my-subscription) says you can still log in after the plan expires, but "all dynamic QR codes will become invalid."

### Uniqode

Uniqode's [subscription article](https://docs.uniqode.com/en/articles/17190918-what-happens-to-my-qr-codes-when-my-subscription-changes-or-ends) says dynamic codes stop working when a trial expires. If you cancel, they keep working until your renewal date and stop after it. Your account and codes are kept, so reactivating brings them back. Downgrading keeps codes running only if you stay within the smaller plan's limit.

### Scanova

Scanova's [billing docs](https://docs.scanova.io/billing/trial-and-expiry) say dynamic codes are deactivated when the 14-day trial ends, and that a lapsed paid subscription works the same way. Nothing is deleted: subscribing again makes the same codes work at the destinations they already had.

### QRCodeChimp

Its [pricing page FAQ](https://www.qrcodechimp.com/pricing) says the free plan's dynamic codes share 1,000 scans a month, and once that's used, scans are paused until the next month. If a paid plan isn't renewed, the account moves to the free plan and every dynamic code except the last 10 is paused. To cancel renewal, the same FAQ says to email their support team at least a day before the renewal date.

### Hovercode

Hovercode's [cancellation article](https://hovercode.com/help/article/32-if-you-cancel) says cancelling moves you to the free plan. Your first 3 dynamic codes, first 3 short links and first multi-link page stay active. Anything beyond that goes inactive but isn't deleted, and anything on your own custom domain stops working. Resubscribing turns it all back on.

### Flowcode

Flowcode's help centre [says](https://help.flowcode.com/en/articles/7061611-will-my-codes-pages-expire) its codes never expire and have unlimited scans on every plan. Its [downgrade article](https://help.flowcode.com/en/articles/6927459-what-happens-to-my-account-and-codes-when-i-downgrade) says that on the free plan you keep access to your 2 most recent codes, and all other codes keep sending people to the destination you last set. The same article also mentions restoring codes "deleted upon downgrading", so if you depend on this, ask Flowcode to confirm what happens to your specific codes.

### Scanstone

Our [terms](/terms) say cancelling, downgrading or going over a plan limit will not disable existing redirects. The free plan includes 3 dynamic codes or scst.one short links in total, and [Pro](/pricing) is $9 USD a month or $79 USD a year for 500. If you cancel Pro, you lose the Pro extras and your printed codes keep working. You cancel yourself from the billing portal, with no email to send.

Redirects do end if you delete the code or your account, or if a code breaks our acceptable use rules. If Scanstone ever shuts down for good, the terms commit us to at least 90 days' email notice and a window to export your data. Like any dynamic code, ours also depend on our redirect service and your destination staying up.

## Before you cancel anything

1. **Check what kind of code you printed.** Scan it and look at the address before you open it. If it's your own website or file, it's static and cancelling won't touch it. If it's a short link on the QR company's domain, it's dynamic and depends on them.
2. **Read that company's own policy.** The table above is a summary of what each company published when we checked. Plans change, so check the current help page for the account you actually have.
3. **Find out the date.** Some companies stop codes the day the plan ends, some at the renewal date, some 30 days later. Put the date in your calendar.
4. **Count your codes.** Where a free plan keeps a few codes running, it's usually the first or the most recent few. Know which of your printed codes fall outside that.
5. **Move the important ones first.** For any code on packaging, signs or menus you can't easily reprint, make the replacement and reprint before the cutoff, not after.

If a code has already stopped, our guide on [what to do when a QR code stops working](/blog/qr-code-stopped-working-how-to-fix) walks through the fixes. For a printed code that never needed to be dynamic, the [free static generator](/qr-code-generator) makes one that goes straight to your link with nothing in between.

## Common questions

### Do QR codes stop working when you cancel your subscription?

Static codes don't, because your link is stored in the pattern. Dynamic codes often do. QRCG by Bitly, QR TIGER, Uniqode and Scanova all say paid dynamic codes stop after the plan ends. Hovercode, QRCodeChimp and Flowcode keep some codes running on their free plans, and Scanstone keeps all existing redirects running.

### Can I get my QR codes back after cancelling?

Usually only by paying the same company again. QRCG, Uniqode, Scanova and Hovercode all say resubscribing brings existing codes back. A different QR company can't revive them, because the short link inside the code belongs to the company that made it.

### Will my free trial QR codes keep working?

At QRCG by Bitly, Uniqode and Scanova, dynamic codes made during the trial stop working when the trial ends unless you upgrade. Static codes made during the trial keep working.

### Does Scanstone turn off codes if I stop paying?

No. Cancelling, downgrading or going over a plan limit doesn't disable existing redirects. That's in section 2 of our terms. Redirects end if you delete the code or your account.
