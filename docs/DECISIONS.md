| 17 | 11-13 | No fixed expiry on unclaimed guest links | A date or 30-day timer would fight the never-expire promise. Links keep working with no end date. Copy tells visitors that without an account it is hard to honour that promise, and a free account keeps the link safe and editable. |
| 18 | 11-13 | Abuse takedowns use `disabled_at` and `/link-unavailable` | Operators can disable a slug without deleting it. Visitors get a friendly page, not a bare 404. Missing slugs still go to `/?missing-code=1`. Existing account and guest redirects are otherwise unchanged. |
| 19 | 11-13 | Guest create is capped at 5 per day per IP and per guest cookie, fail closed | Limits casual flooding. A limiter error refuses the create rather than opening a hole. Destination validation plus an empty domain-blocklist hook are the other abuse controls. No Safe Browsing integration. |
| 20 | 11-13 | Claiming on sign-up or log-in moves every non-disabled guest link onto the account; over the free limit they stay live but destination edits are blocked | Users should not lose links they just made. Redirects keep working (the covenant). They can delete down to the limit or upgrade to edit destinations. |
| 21 | 11-13 | Before a guest link is created, visitors must accept a versioned notice in an accessible modal; the server stores the timestamp and wording version and rejects creates that lack them | The never-expire caveat has to be explicit, not buried after the fact. Primary action is "Create a free account". "Continue without an account" stays disabled until the checkbox is checked. Stored version (`v1`) stays accurate if the wording later changes. |
