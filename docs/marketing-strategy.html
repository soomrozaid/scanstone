Core message

“Print it once. Update it anytime.”
New menu. New offer. Same QR code. Change destinations after printing, count scans, and keep redirects working after cancellation.

Seller baseline: Scanstone supplies static generators, editable redirects, short links, and scan/click analytics. The free plan includes 3 dynamic codes or links; Pro is $9/month or $79/year for 500. Its cancellation policy does not gate redirects on billing. Source: current repository, pricing, and terms. It does not host restaurant menus, take orders, or provide the enterprise features advertised by larger platforms.

Visual direction: retain warm paper, green accents, and editorial typography. Put a recognizable table card and an interactive destination example above the fold. Keep one primary signup action and a useful demo. Use real product evidence before adding testimonials or customer logos.

Positioning read: lead on the cancellation policy for a simple print workflow. Broader features are a pressure point against platform vendors. Free static generation is useful acquisition, not a unique capability. These are strategic inferences from the documented product scope.

Say: “If your menu URL changes, update the destination without replacing your printed code.” Clarify: whether they control a stable URL, need analytics, and already have printed codes. Prove: show the same sample scanning before and after a destination change, with the pricing and terms available.

Guidance

1. Earn useful traffic

Improve the free generator and the new restaurant page. Explain static versus dynamic, trial expiry, and the actual setup process. Avoid creating a separate page for every keyword variation.

2. Show the product

Publish one original screen recording weekly. Repurpose it into a short video and a useful founder post. Explore permission-based print-shop partnerships after observing real setups.

3. Automate the routine

Weekly Search Console brief → one prioritized improvement → draft content → owner review → publish → measure. Use the existing stack before adding paid software.
